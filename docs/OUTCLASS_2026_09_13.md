# Current contact-engine comparison, 2026-09-13

This table compares current measurements with archived own-engine values and
predeclared capability gates. It is not a head-to-head benchmark against an
external engine. No external executable ran these scenes. A numerical WIN
against our archived implementation must not be presented as defeating a
referent on an identical problem.

The [Box3D feature list](https://github.com/erincatto/box3d) documents cross-platform
determinism and multiple collision/joint primitives. The [AVBD project](https://graphics.cs.utah.edu/research/projects/avbd/)
describes an augmented-Lagrangian extension, implicit-Euler convergence,
hard constraints and high-stiffness-ratio cases. Neither source makes our
box stacks identical to its demonstrations. A springs/joints claim cannot
be scored using a solver scene without springs/joints.

Fresh evidence: `reports/outclass_rescore_probe_l4.json`, two isolated Modal
L4 workers with Warp1.13.0. J=fixed-point Jacobi; G=colored device-manifold
with captured solve; P=captured pair chunks; C=ColorCacheContactEngine.
All shared boxes measure0.3/0.3/0.2, friction0.5, dt1/240. Physical cases use
40 velocity/10 position iterations; speed uses40/20 with10 warm/30 timed steps.
M4 uses200 steps and the original2/4/6/8/10/20/40 velocity sweep, with validity
recorded at each budget. Complete state histories after every physical step
repeat exactly for all four configurations. Timing is measured without the
history observer, synchronized at both bounds; complete final states repeat.

WIN means an explicitly tested target improves over our banked result;
TIE means a demonstrated capability is retained; LOSE means the tested target
fails; UNFAIR means no comparable fresh measurement supports scoring it.
These labels never establish a head-to-head win on absent referent runs.
